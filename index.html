Welcome to Utkarsh 5.0 at Tripura Institute of Technology

As the first and only state-run government engineering institute in the state, Tripura Institute of Technology (TIT), Narsingarh, stands at the forefront of technical education and youth culture in the region. This year, we are thrilled to announce the return of our grand annual techno-cultural festival: Utkarsh 5.0.

We invite forward-thinking brands and organizations to partner with us as we bring together the brightest minds and the most energetic talents for a multi-day celebration of technology, art, and innovation.

State-Run Engineering Hub

TIT Narsingarh leads Tripura's technological innovation, fostering engineering excellence across disciplines for decades.

Techno-Cultural Fusion

A multi-day extravaganza bridging combat robotics, aerial drone racing, high-octane musical concerts, and stunt shows.

Corporate Synergy
