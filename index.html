Premier State Institute

Welcome to Utkarsh 5.0 at Tripura Institute of Technology

As the first and only state-run government engineering institute in the state, Tripura Institute of Technology (TIT), Narsingarh, stands at the forefront of technical education and youth culture in the region. This year, we are thrilled to announce the return of our grand annual techno-cultural festival: Utkarsh 5.0.

We invite forward-thinking brands and organizations to partner with us as we bring together the brightest minds and the most energetic talents for a multi-day celebration of technology, art, and innovation.

Unmatched Statewide Reach

TIT is a melting pot of talent. Our campus draws a highly diverse and engaged demographic of students from every major and minor city, town, and district across Tripura. Partnering with us guarantees your brand direct, meaningful exposure to the state's most dynamic youth market.

A Legacy of Excellence

Utkarsh has consistently set the benchmark for college festivals in the region. Building on the monumental success of our previous editions—which were proudly supported by industry giants like Triumph—Utkarsh 5.0 is scaling up to be our most spectacular event yet.
